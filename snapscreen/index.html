Support

We're here to help. SnapScreen turns your screenshots into polished images with device frames, backgrounds and tools to crop and hide sensitive details. If something isn't working, or you have a question or an idea, write to us. We read every message.

Email support

How do I send feedback or report a problem?

Email support@silverai.com. Telling us your device model and iOS version helps us help you faster.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. Editing happens entirely on your device. We never receive your screenshots. The Privacy Policy says exactly what the app does send and receive.

Why does SnapScreen ask for access to Photos?

To let you choose screenshots to edit and to save the finished image back to your library. You can change this in iOS Settings → SnapScreen → Photos at any time.

Does “hide sensitive text” catch everything?

It finds text on your device and blurs or covers it, but automatic detection can miss text — for example small, stylised, rotated or low-contrast text. Always check the result before you share it.

Why do frames and backgrounds need an internet connection?

The app downloads the list of frames and backgrounds, and their images, when you first open them, then keeps them on your device. Only a standard download request is made; none of your pictures are involved.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us. Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions to change or cancel. Changed device or reinstalled? Tap Restore purchases in the app.
