Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Your screenshots never leave your device. SnapScreen edits them on your phone and saves the result to your photo library. The app only downloads frames and backgrounds, and sends anonymous analytics. There is no account.

1 Who we are

SnapScreen (“the app”) is developed by Silver AI (“we”, “us”). You can reach us any time at support@silverai.com.

2 What is sent and received

  • Your screenshots and images are never uploaded. Everything — framing, cropping, backgrounds, and finding sensitive text to hide — runs on your device.
  • The app downloads its list of frames and backgrounds, and their images, from our asset server. This is an ordinary download request: it carries no pictures, no account and no advertising identifier, though like any web request it exposes your IP address to the server.
  • The app fetches its remote settings from Firebase Remote Config.
  • Anonymous analytics events (see below).

3 What stays on your device

  • Your screenshots and the edited images.
  • The text the app finds in your screenshots, used only to offer to hide it.
  • Your edits, recent history and settings.

None of this is uploaded or visible to us. Deleting the app removes the data stored by the app; images you saved to your photo library stay there, because they belong to you.

4 No accounts, no ads

SnapScreen has no sign-in and no user account. We do not build a profile of you, we do not sell or share data with data brokers, and the app contains no advertising and no cross-app tracking.

5 Analytics

The app uses Google Firebase for anonymous usage analytics so we can see which features are used and fix what breaks. This can include app usage and feature interactions, device type, iOS and app version, country or region, and crash or performance data. It never includes your screenshots or the text in them. Firebase processes this data as our service provider, under its own privacy terms.

6 Purchases

SnapScreen’s premium subscription, offered weekly or yearly, is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app learns only whether your subscription is active, its type and when it expires.

7 Device permissions we request

  • Photos (read and write) — to let you choose screenshots to edit and to save the finished image to your library.

You can change this in iOS Settings → SnapScreen at any time. The app does not request your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

8 Your control over your data

  • Delete any edit from within the app, or uninstall the app to remove its stored data.
  • Revoke Photos access in iOS Settings.
  • To ask about analytics data associated with your use of the app, email support@silverai.com.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or California, you have the rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your data. Analytics is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the app, and the subscription on the basis of the contract with you. We hold no personal data that identifies you.

9 Children

SnapScreen is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10 Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above and post the new version at this URL.

11 Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@silverai.com.
