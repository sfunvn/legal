Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Your photos and videos never leave your device. When you capture one, TimeCam registers a small record — a code, the time, the place, your device model, the camera settings and a fingerprint of the file — so the stamp can be checked later. There is no account, and we do not sell your data.

1 Who we are

TimeCam (“the app”) is developed by Silver AI (“we”, “us”). You can reach us any time at support@silverai.com.

2 What is sent off your device

Each time you take a photo or record a video, the app sends a capture record to our Firebase backend so the stamp can be verified later. It contains:

  • a randomly generated photo code (like A1B2-C3D4-E5F6);
  • the capture time and time zone, and a trusted server time we use to check your clock;
  • the location shown on your stamp — coordinates and the address text — when location is available;
  • your device model, the camera settings (aperture, ISO, shutter speed, focal length) and the resolution;
  • a hash of the file — a SHA-256 fingerprint, not the picture; for a video, also its duration.

The photo or video itself is not sent. The record carries no name, no account, no contacts and no advertising identifier. The app proves it is the genuine TimeCam to our backend using Firebase App Check.

3 Looking up a code

The record exists so that a stamp can be checked: anyone who has your photo code can look up its record — including the time, place and device model above. Share a stamped photo only with people you are happy to give that to. We do not publish a list of codes.

4 What stays on your device

  • Your photos and videos, before and after they are stamped.
  • Everything in Private Cam: its pictures, folders, notes and settings.
  • Your templates, stamp settings and preferences.

There is no cloud backup. This data is removed when you delete an item, or when you uninstall the app.

5 Ads and tracking

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, the app first shows Google's consent form, and on iOS it may ask permission to track you across apps. If you say no, ads are not personalised. AdMob may collect device information such as your advertising identifier — see Google’s Privacy Policy.

6 Analytics, crash reports and settings

The app uses Google Firebase for usage analytics, crash reports, performance monitoring and remote configuration, so we can see which features are used, fix what breaks, and deliver updated templates and settings. These are counts and events with technical details such as the device model, iOS version and app version. Firebase processes this data as our service provider. They never include your photos or videos.

7 Purchases

TimeCam’s subscription is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app only learns whether your subscription is active.

8 Device permissions we request

  • Camera — to take photos and videos.
  • Microphone — only to record sound with a video.
  • Photos — to save stamped pictures and to let you pick a photo to edit.
  • Location (while using the app) — to print the place on your stamp. Optional.
  • Tracking — to ask whether ads may be personalised. Optional.

You can grant or revoke each of these in iOS Settings → TimeCam at any time. If you refuse one, the features that need it are unavailable; the rest keep working.

9 Your control over your data

  • Delete any picture from within the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Turn off location, or ad tracking, in iOS Settings.
  • To see or delete the capture records for your codes, email support@silverai.com with the codes.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or California, you have the rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your data, and to data portability where it applies. Use the email above to exercise them. Our capture records are keyed by photo code and are not linked to your identity, so we need the code to find a record.

10 Children

TimeCam is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above and post the new version at this URL.
