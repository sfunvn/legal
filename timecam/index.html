Email support

How do I send feedback or report a problem?

Email support@silverai.com. Telling us your device model and iOS version helps us help you faster.

What leaves my phone when I take a photo?

Your photo or video stays on your device. When you capture, the app registers a small record with our servers — a random code, the time, the place, your device model, the camera settings and a fingerprint (hash) of the file — so the stamp can be checked later. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is sent.

Why does TimeCam ask for my location?

To print the place on your stamp. It is optional: if you say no, the app still works, just without a place on the stamp. You can change this in iOS Settings → TimeCam → Location at any time.

Why does TimeCam ask for the camera, microphone and Photos?

Camera to take photos and video, Microphone only to record sound with a video, and Photos to save your stamped pictures and to let you pick one to edit. You can change any of these in iOS Settings → TimeCam.

Where are my Private Cam pictures stored?

On your device only, in TimeCam's own storage. There is no cloud backup and no account, so deleting the app removes them. Export anything you want to keep to Photos first.

Can I use a TimeCam stamp as legal proof?

Treat it as a helpful record, not a guarantee. The stamp uses your phone's clock and location sensors, which can be wrong or changed. TimeCam is not a notary or certification service.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us. Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions to change or cancel. Changed device or reinstalled? Tap Restore purchases in the app.
