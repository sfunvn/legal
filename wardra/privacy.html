Privacy Policy

1 Who we are

Wardra (“the app”) is developed by Silver AI (“we”, “us”). You can reach us any time at support@silverai.com.

2 What is sent off your device

Wardra cannot cut a garment out of its background on your phone, so when you add an item, one thing is sent to our background-removal service:

  • A single downscaled JPEG of that one garment photo, and optionally a short category hint such as “shirt”. Nothing else accompanies it.
  • The service processes the image and returns the cut-out; the image is not kept. The cut-out is stored on your device.
  • The request carries no name, no account, no email, no device identifier and no advertising identifier. It identifies the app and its version, not you, so two requests cannot be tied to the same person by anything the app sends.
  • The original, full-resolution photograph is never uploaded — only the downscaled copy of the garment, and only for removal.

Background removal runs on every item you add, on both the free and Pro tiers. If it fails, you can save the photo un-cut and nothing is sent again.

3 Adding an item from a shop link

“Thêm từ link” opens a shop page in a browser inside the app. That is your own web traffic: the shop receives a normal request from your device for the page you asked for, exactly as Safari would send it.

  • The app adds nothing of yours to that request — no item, no photo, no measurement, and no Wardra identifier. No request of ours is made at all.
  • From the page, the app takes one image URL — the picture of the garment, which you confirm. Never a name, a price, a cart, or anything you type into that page.
  • The browsing session is ephemeral: cookies and the page are discarded when you leave, and nothing about the page — not the URL history, not its other images — is stored by us.
  • The picked image then follows the ordinary path above: saved to your device, and sent for background removal.

A shop can still see and log a visit from your device, as any website can. What the app can promise is that it adds nothing to what the shop already sees.

4 What stays on your device

Everything else, in the app's own storage:

  • Every item — its name, brand, category, colour, season, occasion, favourite, price, the link you saved, and the cut-out.
  • Every outfit, every wear log and your calendar.
  • Your three body measurements and the reading derived from them. These are the most sensitive data in the app and there is no code path that transmits them — not to us, not to analytics, not anywhere.
  • Your photographs and your settings.

None of this is uploaded and none of it is visible to us. There is no account, no sync and no cloud backup: this data is removed when you delete an item, or when you uninstall the app.

5 No accounts, no profile of you

Wardra has no sign-in and no user account. We do not build a profile of you, we do not sell or share data with data brokers, and the app contains no advertising and no cross-app tracking. It never asks for tracking permission and does not use the advertising identifier.

6 Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase for anonymous usage analytics and crash reports, so we can see which screens are used and fix what breaks. These are counts and events — that a screen was opened, that an item was added, that the app crashed — with the device model and iOS version. Firebase processes this data as our service provider.

They never carry your content. Never an item name, brand, colour, price, photo, shop link or outfit — and not the category either, because a category histogram is a picture of your wardrobe. Never your measurements or your body-shape reading, not as a value and not as a range. The only thing recorded about you as a person is whether your subscription is active.

7 Purchases

Wardra Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app stores only whether your subscription is active, on your device.

8 Device permissions we request

  • Camera — only to photograph a garment when you tap “Chụp ảnh”. If you refuse, the app falls back to picking from your library; nothing becomes a dead end.
  • Photo library — asked only when you want to select several photos at once. Picking a single photo uses iOS's own picker and needs no permission at all, so that path keeps working if you refuse.

Wardra does not ask for notifications, location, contacts or tracking. You can change any permission in iOS Settings → Wardra at any time.

9 Your control over your data

  • Delete any item, outfit or wear log from within the app.
  • Edit or clear your measurements at any time, in Cá nhân ▸ Số đo.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its locally stored data.

Because we hold no account and no copy of your wardrobe, there is nothing for us to export or delete on your behalf — it is all in your hands.

10 Children

Wardra is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
